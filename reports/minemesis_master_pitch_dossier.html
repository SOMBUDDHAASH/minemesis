1. Executive Summary: What Is Minemesis?

Minemesis is an autonomous historical simulation engine engineered to solve the hardest unsolved problem in procedural gaming: generating centuries of deep, coherent, interconnected civilizational lore that feels authentically human. While procedural engines traditionally generate static terrain or shallow name-tables, and Large Language Models (LLMs) hallucinate disconnected stories, Minemesis simulates an entire living society from first principles: marriages, births, resource pressures, subjective beliefs, wrongful feuds, religious schisms, and literature. Every book found on a shelf in a Minecraft village can be traced back through an unbroken, verifiable causal chain of ancestors, tragedies, treaties, and eyewitness accounts.

2. The Fundamental Problem with LLMs in World Generation

Why Large Language Models Fail at Deep History

  • Catastrophic Hallucination & Inconsistency: An LLM cannot maintain thousands of interconnected family trees and causal threads across centuries without generating impossible contradictions (e.g. a soldier avenging a grandfather who died after him).
  • Astronomical Token Economics: Simulating 100 years of village life (thousands of births, conversations, feuds) with GPT-4 or Claude costs hundreds to thousands of dollars per world, making it commercially unviable for consumer games.
  • No Ground Truth or Invariants: LLMs cannot enforce mathematical conservation ($P = P_0 + B - D$) or ensure economic viability of trade routes.

The Minemesis Algorithmic Breakthrough

  • Zero Hallucination & Absolute Causal Truth: Every historical event is an immutable node in a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG), validated against strict causal grammar rules. Cause always precedes effect.
  • 100% Free, Offline & Instant: Runs entirely on consumer hardware in Python/C-extensions in seconds, with zero external API calls and zero cloud subscription overhead.
  • Direct Vanilla Minecraft Integration: Compiles procedural manuscripts into official Minecraft Written Books (NBT JSON tags), allowing players to hold, read, and trade real historical books in-game.

The Chronological Evolution: Every Decision & Pivot

Minemesis did not happen by accident. It is the result of disciplined, iterative engineering, rigorous benchmarking, and honest pivots when assumptions failed under pressure. Below is the complete record of every architectural decision, the bottlenecks discovered, and how each challenge was decisively solved.

Phase 0: Foundational Causal Grammar & Demographic Conservation (M0)

The Core Decision: Establish immutable physical laws before adding any narrative fluff. Zero demographic drift ($P = P_0 + B - D$) was made a hard invariant: population at tick $t$ must exactly equal initial population plus total births minus total deaths. Every event must cite legal causal parents according to a formal grammar DAG.

The Outcome: Completely eliminated "ghost populations", uncaused spontaneous events, and demographic collapse. Validated across 10,000+ test years.

Phase 0.5.1: Settlement Topography & Ecological Spacing

The Alteration in Idea: Early prototypes spawned villages randomly, leading to overlapping settlements, trade route trivialization, and unrealistic immediate extinctions.

The Engineering Solution: Enforced a strict 75-tile minimum spatial distance constraint across a 400×400 tile world, procedural placement of 3 to 7 settlements across 4 distinct terrain archetypes (Farmland, Forest, Mining, River), economic viability checks, and a formal settlement collapse threshold ($\le 4$ citizens).

Phase 0.5.2: The Dual-Seed Architecture & Crockford Base32

The Problem: World terrain and civilizational history were initially bound to a single integer seed. Changing one character altered the entire continent's mountains.

The Engineering Solution: Decoupled world generation into SeedPair(world_seed, chronicle_seed). A world's geography is fixed by the World Seed; its history, leaders, feuds, and religions are driven by the Chronicle Seed. Users can test "Same Map, New History" with one click. Encoded into typo-safe, hyphenated Crockford Base32 strings with Luhn check digits (e.g. WB84Q-PGW6-H8FX5:HBSGH-DGGJ-CE46D:V0:6).

Phase 0.5.3: The Psychology Layer, Wrongful Grudges & Truth Revelation

The Core Innovation: Replaced omniscient agent logic with an epistemic belief system. When citizen $A$ dies in a forestry accident with citizen $B$, an observer with high suspicion does not record an "Accident"—they form an inferred belief of "Homicide" by $B$. This sparks a blood feud. Decades later, an eyewitness can communicate the truth, triggering a formal Revelation event and ending the feud with a negotiated peace treaty.

The Turning Point: The Great Architecture Review & The 7.1 GB Bottleneck

In October 2026, formal profiling revealed a catastrophic bottleneck: At Year 100, the simulation consumed 7.1 GB of RAM with 2,850,000 active beliefs! Distant historical citizens from 90 years ago who died as teenage farmers were still retaining 40+ individual belief objects in RAM. Mathematical projections showed that a 4,000-year simulation would require 75 GB to 350 GB of RAM, guaranteeing Out-Of-Memory crashes.

"The critical question was posed: Should we abandon Python and rewrite the simulation in Rust, C++, or Go? The verdict: DO NOT switch languages. Redesign memory and epistemic storage immediately. The CPU runtime wasn't the problem (runs finished in 2 minutes); the architecture was accidentally implementing 'every villager remembers everything forever' instead of real human civilizational knowledge."

Milestone 0.5.4: The 4-Tier Memory Lifecycle & Epistemic Compaction

The Solution: Structured human memory into 4 operational tiers: Tier 0 (Living, full simulation), Tier 1 (Recent dead within 10 years, preserved for vengeance/inheritance), Tier 2 (Compressed Historical Citizens in HistoricalCitizenArchive), and Tier 3 (Civilizational History: books, settlement collective memory, cultural myths). Distant ephemeral beliefs are aggressively compacted every 120 ticks, while protecting feuds and leaders.

Milestone 0.5.4 Addendum: Relational Lore Compression & Lazy Synthesis

The Discovery: 1,817 procedural books were consuming 6.4 MB of raw page strings in RAM, and EventLog payloads were duplicating character names thousands of times.

The Solution: 1) Stripped redundant strings from event payloads; 2) Created centralized string intern pool (EntityTable.intern); 3) Converted all 9 book generators to deterministic lazy page renderers via KeyedRNG. Books consume 0 bytes of page text in RAM during simulation until explicitly viewed or exported!

PROJECT MINEMESIS • FEATURES & CAPABILITIES
COMPREHENSIVE PRODUCT INVENTORY (MILESTONE 0.5.4)

Product Features & Technical Capabilities

Minemesis delivers a complete, cohesive simulation and presentation stack. Below is the technical breakdown of all major capabilities operating in the engine today.

1. Causal Graph & Deep Ancestry Tracing

Every event records explicit, validated causes. The engine can trace backward from any modern event (e.g. a peace treaty in Year 100) all the way back to the root forestry accident in Year 12 that triggered the original grudge.

  • Acyclic DAG Integrity: Strictly zero circular causal loops.
  • Backward & Forward Inspection: Query both "Why did this happen?" and "What were the consequences of this event?".
  • Deepest Conflict Path Analysis: Automatically identifies the longest and most dramatic historical feud chains in the world.

2. Epistemic Psychology & Gossip Network

Agents experience reality subjectively. Rather than sharing a global knowledge base, each citizen constructs an individual belief ledger.

  • Three Belief Sources: Witnessed (direct observation, high confidence), Told (hearsay via communication/trade routes), and Inferred (biased deductions based on character suspicion).
  • Belief Confidence & Decay: Beliefs decay over time unless reinforced, enabling forgotten grievances and exhaustion peace.
  • Reappraisal upon Revelation: When an eyewitness reveals the truth, agents update their beliefs, form emotional reappraisal memories, and formally reconcile.

3. Living Religion & Sacred Geography

Religion in Minemesis is not a static lore blurb; it is an active simulation agent driving human behavior and geopolitical alliances.

  • Theological Tenets & Commandments: Emergent moral virtues, taboos, and ritual duties derived from settlement founder traits.
  • Sanctuaries & Pilgrimages: Holy sites discovered at natural landmarks; citizens embark on holy pilgrimages, forming spiritual memories.
  • Syncretism, Heresy & Schisms: Cohabiting faiths exchange doctrines; conservative priests declare heresies; ideological disputes cause sects to break away.

4. Procedural Literature Engine (9 Genres)

Elders, chiefs, priests, and travelers author authentic manuscripts from actual historical events using procedural grammatical templates (no LLMs).

  • 9 Distinct Genres: Family Histories, Personal Journals, Religious Scriptures, Myth Collections, Historical Chronicles, Local Folklore, Legal Records, Travelogues, and Biographies.
  • Subjective Author Perspective: Books reflect the author's personal biases and beliefs rather than omniscient ground truth.
  • Library Repositories & War Damage: Manuscripts stored across Town Archives and Temple Libraries; border raids burn libraries and corrupt parchment text.

5. Direct Minecraft Integration: Written Books & NBT Tags

Minemesis procedural books are directly compiled into standard Minecraft Written Book NBT JSON structures. Any book generated during centuries of simulation can be exported and spawned into vanilla Minecraft with a single command.

Pros, Cons & Strategic Assessment

An honest assessment of our current strengths, engineering trade-offs, and remaining technical debt.

Technical Pros & Competitive Advantages

  • 100% Deterministic & Auditable: The identical seed pair produces the byte-identical history, event for event, on any operating system (Windows, Linux, macOS).
  • Zero Hallucination & Zero API Incurrence: No monthly OpenAI/Anthropic token bills; no servers required; complete offline privacy.
  • Mathematically Verified: 100% automated test coverage across 108 regression suites enforcing physical conservation and causal grammar.
  • True Narrative Depth: Feuds are motivated by authentic misunderstandings; books reflect subjective human biases; religions split organically over doctrine.

Current Limitations & Technical Debt

  • Python Single-Thread CPU Bound: Simulating 4,000 years continuously takes ~35–45 minutes on a single CPU thread. Mitigation: Multi-core multiprocessing across settlement regions, or ahead-of-time batch generation.
  • Topological Spatial Model: Settlements currently exist on a 2D coordinate grid with Euclidean distance rather than full 3D voxel heightmaps. Mitigation: Milestone 1 world mapper translates 2D settlement coordinates directly into Minecraft world biomes.
  • GUI Framework: Desktop viewer uses Tkinter for zero-dependency simplicity. While functional, it is not web-hosted or GPU-rendered.
