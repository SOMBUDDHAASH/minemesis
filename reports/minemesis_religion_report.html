1. Executive Summary & Paradigm Shift

In conventional simulation games and RPG world generators, religion is typically modeled as a static political faction, an institutional state church, or an arbitrary diplomatic opinion modifier. Such models suffer from acute Abrahamic institutional bias—presupposing that religion fundamentally consists of a single prophet, a dogmatic canon, a rigid church hierarchy, heresy persecutions, and Protestant-style reformations.

The Minemesis Religion Engine represents a ground-up paradigm shift. It implements a unified, non-dogmatic simulation architecture capable of natively modeling Dharmic traditions (Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism), East Asian philosophies (Daoism, Shinto, Confucianism), African traditional cosmologies (Yoruba, Egyptian), European polytheisms (Celtic, Norse, Hellenic), Indigenous animisms, and vernacular syncretic folk faiths without forcing any of them into ecclesiastical church mechanics.

The Fundamental Religion Equation:
Beliefs + Virtues + Myths + Rituals + Sacred Places + Cultural Practices + Historical Memory = Religion
Religion in Minemesis is NOT a faction, a kingdom, a guild, or a denomination. Religion is a living collection of ideas. Ideas spread across borders, combine through syncretism, mutate through generations, drift across geography, and shape civilizational culture.

4. Core Philosophy: Complete Decoupling of Religion and Politics

In historical reality, political conflict and religious ideology interact as distinct forces. Two villages sharing the identical river faith may raid each other over iron mines or water rights; conversely, settlements adhering to radically divergent theological worldviews may establish vibrant trade pacts and dynastic marriage alliances.

What Religion IS NOT in Minemesis ANTI-PATTERNS
  • NOT a Kingdom or Faction: Political loyalty and religious conviction are completely separate state vectors.
  • NOT a Single Village: Multiple villages can share a faith, split a faith, or develop local syncretisms.
  • NOT a Monolithic Monotheism: Polytheism, animism, and philosophical ethics are first-class peers.
  • NOT a Rigid Church State: Religious movements emerge organically from societal grief, triumphs, and visions.
What Religion IS in Minemesis LIVING IDEAS
  • A Living Cognitive Filter: Modifies how subjective perceptions are converted into enduring beliefs.
  • A Moral & Ethical Framework: Rewards virtuous actions with community standing and penalizes taboos.
  • A Cultural Memory Reservoir: Preserves ancestral heroics through oral tales, legends, and civilizational myths.
  • A Dynamic Geography Network: Consecrates sacred groves, rivers, and peaks, stimulating pilgrimages.

10. Religious Tension & The Sectarian Paradox (R1.2)

In historical sociology, religious conflicts are rarely most intense between completely foreign faiths. Rather, internal schisms, reformed sects, and divergent heresies frequently evoke intense enmity. Minemesis operationalizes this through ReligiousRelationshipType:

Relationship Classification TYPOLOGY
  • SharedFaith (Friction 0.0): Mutual communion; shared sacred festivals and temples.
  • SimilarFaith (Friction 15.0): High theological kinship; cordial diplomatic views.
  • HostileDoctrine (Friction 40.0): Incompatible core ethics (e.g. Pacifism vs Holy War).
  • DivergentSect (Friction 65.0): Breakaway heresy or schism; highest mutual bitterness.
Settlement Tension Formulation ALGORITHM

For a settlement with population \(N\) and resident faiths \(f_i\) with proportions \(p_i\): 

T_settlement = ∑ (p_i * p_j * Friction(f_i, f_j))

15. The Heresy & Reformation Lifecycle Engine

Religious divergence proceeds through four distinct, interrelated historical phases:

Phase 1: Heterodoxy & Schism DIVERGENCE

When an adherent develops ideological discontent in a settlement with ≥4 co-religionists, check_and_declare_heresy evaluates doctrinal fracture. A novel theological trait is adopted, generating HeresyDeclared followed by ReligiousSchism. A new child religion is instantiated, inheriting scripture and traditions while splitting the local population.

Phase 2: Counter-Reformation ORTHODOXY

Mother religions do not remain passive when fractured. With probability COUNTER_REFORMATION_CHANCE (5%), the mother faith launches a counter-reformation. evaluate_counter_reformation purges vulnerable doctrines, re-asserts orthodoxy, increases cleric missionary fervor, and emits ReligiousReform.

Phase 3: Saint Cult Conversions POPULAR DEVOTION

Saints and martyrs do not remain dead abstractions. Every month, process_saint_cult_conversions evaluates local townsfolk venerating deceased saints. Miraculous tales attributed to saint relics trigger spontaneous lay conversions into the saint's parent tradition.

Phase 4: Sectarian Reunification CONVERGENCE

When splinter sects suffer catastrophic population decline or when doctrinal distance shrinks, evaluate_sect_reunification evaluates armistice. If compatible, the splinter faith is re-absorbed into the parent tradition, consolidating adherents and emitting SectReunification.

26. Doctrinal Borrowing Without Conversion (R1.5.4)

Faiths frequently absorb philosophical ideas, ethical virtues, or artistic rituals from neighboring traditions without converting or merging. Through evaluate_doctrinal_borrowing:

  • Recipient faiths adopt non-conflicting traits from donor faiths (e.g., an animist forest cult adopting charity_obligation from an urban commercial faith).
  • Lineage is formally tracked in borrowed_traits = {trait_id: donor_religion_id}.
  • Emits DoctrinalBorrowing into the EventLog.

27. Faith Convergence Metric: Jaccard Similarity (R1.5.5)

To measure long-term cultural and theological alignment between cohabiting religions, the engine computes the Jaccard Trait Similarity Index: $$J(A, B) = rac{|Traits(A) \cap Traits(B)|}{|Traits(A) \cup Traits(B)|}$$

When \(J(A, B) \ge 0.65\), the faiths have converged so closely that inter-faith tension drops to zero, facilitating formal armistice, mutual recognition, or institutional synthesis.

39. Current Architectural Boundaries & Limitations

In accordance with Minemesis engineering rigor, all current boundaries are explicitly documented:

1. Pairwise Trade Corridors BOUNDARY

Trade route evangelism and cultural diffusion currently operate across direct pairwise connections between adjacent settlements. Full multi-hop supply lines and maritime caravans will be modeled in future economic network passes.

2. Single Primary Faith per Character BOUNDARY

While settlements and religions support multi-tradition syncretism, individual characters adhere to one primary faith identity at a time. Multi-faith participation (e.g. practicing Shinto at birth and Buddhism at death) can be introduced in future lifecycle passes.

3. Kinship & Clan Lineages Frozen BOUNDARY

Family structures currently track nuclear relationships (parents, spouses, children). Deep multi-generational clan trees, bloodline claims, and dynastic alliances are deferred to Milestone 1.

4. Headless Execution Invariant BOUNDARY

The simulation remains completely headless. No voxel meshing, graphics rendering, networking, or interactive UI widgets have been added, preserving architectural cleanliness.
