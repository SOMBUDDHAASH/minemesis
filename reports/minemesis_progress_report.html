1. Executive Summary & Core Architectural Thesis

Project Minemesis is an emergent historical simulation architecture designed to generate deep, multi-generational narrative causality in an agent-based voxel world. The system rejects both pre-scripted branching narratives and purely random proc-gen events. Instead, it operates on a foundational pair of principles:

  • The Event Log is Objective Reality: Events (Birth, Death, Raid, Feud, etc.) are immutable, physical ground truth containing exact participants, locations, causes, and authentic intents.
  • Characters React Strictly to Subjective Beliefs: Characters never possess direct access to the Event Log. They operate exclusively through a multi-stage cognitive pipeline: Event → Perception → Belief → Memory → Relationship → Decision → Event.
  • Strict Bit-Level Determinism: The entire world state, cognitive evolution, and history are strictly deterministic. Seed 42 produces identical event logs and SHA-256 digests across 1,200 monthly ticks (100 simulated years).

  • Subjective Distortion & Wrongful Grudges: When a character dies accidentally (e.g. in a forestry mishap), kin outside immediate perception radius form distorted beliefs via gossip, assuming deliberate homicide. This triggers wrongful blood feuds based entirely on misinformation.
  • Witness-Path Revelation & Belief Supersession: Feuds can end when an eyewitness directly reveals the true accidental nature to the instigator. The corrected belief supersedes the prior belief (belief.supersedes = prior.id). Memories based on the superseded belief are discounted, and re-appraisal memories (remorse, relief) heal relationships.
  • Relationships as Derived Views: Affinity, Trust, Hatred, and Fear are not arbitrary stored integers. They are computed dynamically by summing emotional memory valences over time with exponential recency decay.

3. Milestone 0.5.1 Architectural Audits & Resolutions

Following Milestone 0.5, an exhaustive audit was conducted across 50 training runs to uncover hidden edge cases, spurious causal links, and economic imbalances.

Audit 2a: Marriage/Peace Recency Edge CLEANSED

The Legacy Bug: In Milestone 0.5, any marriage taking place within 12 months of a peace treaty cited that Peace as its cause, regardless of who married or which settlements were involved.

Audit Findings across 50 Seeds:

  • Total Marriages in Batch: 2,926
  • Marriages Citing Peace: 590 (20.2%)
  • Genuine Alliance Marriages: 0 (0.0%)
  • Spurious Recency-Based Links: 590 (100.0%)
  • Spurious Birth Tails Descended: 1,617 births falsely linked to peace!

Fix: Eliminated recency fallback. Marriages now cite Peace only when spouses come from opposing belligerent settlements party to a recent armistice (flagged as alliance marriage in payload). Otherwise, root event.

Audit 2d: Zero-Wealth Drain on Ironpeak RESOLVED

The Legacy Bug: In Seed 42, Ironpeak ended 100 years with Wealth = 0, while Riverbend held tens of thousands.

Root Cause Breakdown:

  • Ironpeak had 0 farmland and imported grain at 2 wealth/month.
  • High ore richness (0.8) generated no passive wealth or export income.
  • Exporters received payment even when the buyer had 0 wealth, minting currency from thin air.

Fix: Mines now produce 3 × ore_richness wealth/month; timber produces 1 × forest_density. Import costs are strictly bounded by buyer wealth: cost = min(needed * price, buyer.wealth). Non-negative wealth floor enforced at ≥ 0.

4.1 Overfitting Audit & The Seed 2011 Extinction Edge Case

  • Hold-out A Overfitting Check: Feud counts were exceptionally stable (6.76 vs 7.50, +11.0% diff). However, late-game active feuds jumped from 34% in training to 52% in Hold-out A (+52.9% relative increase), and famines increased from 4.68 to 6.14 (+31.2% relative increase). This variance reflects macro-environmental differences across seed spaces rather than algorithmic overfitting.
  • Hold-out B Hard Failure (Seed 2011 Extinction): In Seed 2011, severe winter famines and protracted raiding forced both Ironpeak and Oakhaven to collapse. Survivors migrated into Riverbend. However, by Year 80, Riverbend's surviving citizens aged beyond fertility without new marriages. The final citizen (Isolde Crowley) died of natural old age in Year 94 Month 2 at age 66. Because the world was completely unpopulated, World Survival received a strict FAIL. This demonstrates total fidelity to audit integrity without cooking results.

8. Limitations & Recommended Next Task

Architectural Limitations OBSERVED
  • Elderly Demographic Senescence (Seed 2011): Prolonged low-fertility periods can cause settlements to age out without replacement.
  • Ghost-Feud Commitments: Active feuds initiated by a settlement prior to collapse continue ticking, attempting phantom raids from empty towns.
  • Kinship Lineage Gaps: Full multi-generational clan and dynasty structures remain unmodeled (scheduled for Milestone 1).
