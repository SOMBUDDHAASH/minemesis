Milestone 1: Kinship Consequences & Epistemic Propagation

Comprehensive technical audit, architectural roadmap, and implementation specification transitioning Minemesis from isolated individual decision-makers to structured familial dynasties, multi-generational feuds, and network-propagated truth revelation.

Core Thesis & Architectural Objective (brief.md Section 5):
"One death produces different reactions across a family, and corrections spread through families."
In Milestone 0.5, drama was driven by isolated individual instigators. Milestone 1 transforms human social networks into living dynasties: kin defend each other, children inherit grievances from parents, blood feuds outlive original participants, and truth revelation cascades through family bonds.

1. Executive Summary & Audit Baseline

Before initiating Milestone 1 code changes, a comprehensive codebase audit was conducted against the canonical specification in brief.md Section 5. The simulation has successfully achieved strict demographic reconciliation (living = initial + births - deaths), deterministic keyed hashing, V0 procedural world generation (10–35 settlements), and complete memory leak isolation.

Existing Foundation (What is Already Built)

  • Individual Family Pointers: Character stores spouse_id, father_id, mother_id, children_ids, and family_id.
  • Cast Generation Tree Seeding: cast_gen.py builds initial elders, couples, and child linkages across 9 naming traditions.
  • Incest Prevention: life_cycle.py enforces nuclear incest checks on marriages.
  • Ad-Hoc Kin Reactions: memory_system.py increases grief/anger when a parent, child, or spouse dies.
  • Chief Succession Children Check: conflict.py inspects children when electing successor chiefs.

Crucial Missing Capabilities (What Must Be Built)

  • Formal Kinship Degree Metric: No generalized kin_degree(a, b) function exists. Extended kin (grandparents, uncles, aunts, cousins, in-laws) are completely unindexed.
  • Reaction Fan-Out Architecture: Reactions currently evaluate globally or per-witness, triggering the 2.8M belief explosion. Need bounded Degree 1 & 2 fan-out + aggregate village belief.
  • Kin-Involved Revenge: Only the single feud instigator can seek revenge. Family members cannot avenge kin.
  • Multi-Generational Grudges: Children do not inherit grudges from deceased parents; feuds die with the instigator.
  • Family Belief-Correction Cascade: Truth revelation currently updates only the instigator. Kin do not receive truth updates.

6. Algorithmic Efficiency & Zero Pointer Cycles

In accordance with brief.md Section 5.1: "Keep explicit parent and spouse pointers for named characters. They are cheap and make revenge and inheritance queries trivial."

  • String-Keyed Traversal: Kinship lookups strictly use string identifiers (e.g. "char_12") into the top-level characters: dict[str, Character] table. No circular Python object references are created.
  • Bounded Search Depth: kin_degree checks only up to 2 genealogical hops. Because parent counts are strictly bounded (2 biological parents) and child counts are capped, Degree 1 and 2 queries evaluate in bounded O(1) time (≤ 24 dictionary lookups).
  • Memoized Query Cache: A lightweight LRU or tick-invalidated cache _kin_cache: dict[tuple[str, str], int] will be maintained to guarantee that frequent monthly relationship updates incur zero CPU overhead.
Acceptance Invariant (test_kin_degree.py):
Must verify 100% precision across complete synthetic family trees: parent-child = 1, sibling = 1, spouse = 1, grandparent-grandchild = 2, uncle/aunt-nephew/niece = 2, first cousins = 2, in-laws = 2, and second cousins = 99 (Degree 3+).

SUBSYSTEM 2: REACTION FAN-OUT

7. Subsystem 2: Reaction Fan-Out & Role-Specific Emotion Modulation

Transforms event perception from an unindexed broadcast to a structured sociological fan-out. Caps discrete memory creation while producing rich, observer-specific psychological reactions.

The Three-Tiered Reaction Fan-Out Architecture

                                  [ NOTABLE EVENT OCCURS (e.g. DEATH) ]
                                                    │
        ┌───────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────┐
        ▼                                           ▼                                           ▼
[ TIER 1: KIN WITHIN DEGREE 1 & 2 ]      [ TIER 2: CLOSE FRIENDS & RIVALS ]          [ TIER 3: SETTLEMENT AGGREGATE ]
- Evaluates kin_degree(deceased, c) ≤ 2  - Friends with Trust > 40.0 (Capped at 3)   - ONE aggregate belief for settlement
- High-intensity individual reactions    - Rivals with Hatred > 40.0 (Capped at 2)   - Background citizens share this record
- Observer-specific emotional roles       - Observer-specific emotional reactions     - ZERO individual memory allocations

Role-Specific Emotion Modulation Matrix

Observer Role Primary Emotion Secondary Emotion Base Intensity
Spouse Grief Despair / Fear 85.0 – 100.0
Child Anger Grief / Revenge Drive 75.0 – 95.0
Parent Grief Anger 80.0 – 95.0
Sibling Hatred Suspicion 65.0 – 85.0
Grandparent / Grandchild Grief Sadness 50.0 – 70.0
Uncle / Aunt / Nephew / Niece Suspicion Grief 45.0 – 65.0
First Cousin / In-Law Suspicion Hatred 40.0 – 60.0
Rival / Enemy Relief Respect / Contempt 60.0 – 90.0

Cognitive Trait Modulation & Decay Parameters

Individual personality traits modulate reaction intensity and longevity:

  • Suspicion (0.0 to 1.0): High suspicion scales Anger and Hatred by up to +25%, and biases the interpretation of accidental deaths toward deliberate murder.
  • Forgiveness (0.0 to 1.0): High forgiveness accelerates memory decay, allowing anger to dissipate faster.
  • Keyed RNG Jitter: Adds small deterministic variance (± 5.0) via keyed_float to ensure family members do not react identically.
Memory Decay Equation:
Intensity(t) = Initial × (1.0 - DecayRate × (1.0 + Forgiveness))^ElapsedTicks

8. Solving the Belief Explosion: Aggregate Settlement Beliefs

In Milestone 0.5, when a character died, all 300+ villagers in a settlement formed identical Belief and Memory records, creating 2.8 million objects.

  • The Milestone 1 Solution: Only Tier 1 kin and Tier 2 close associates (capped at a strict maximum of 10 individuals per event) form individual subjective beliefs and memories.
  • Settlement Public Knowledge: Background residents read from a single shared settlement record: Settlement.public_beliefs: dict[int, AggregateBelief]. This cuts memory allocations by over 85%, solving the primary RAM bottleneck identified in the architecture audit.

10. Multi-Generational Grudge Transmission Engine

How grudges outlive their original instigators across simulated decades:

1. Transmission at Adulthood or Parent Death

When a child reaches adulthood (age 16) or when a parent dies holding an active hatred grudge:

  • The child receives an inherited belief with source=TOLD (told by father/mother).
  • Confidence is attenuated: ChildConfidence = ParentConfidence × 0.70.
  • Hatred memory is formed with reduced initial intensity: ChildHatred = ParentHatred × 0.60.
  • Decay rate is doubled (2x) so inherited grudges fade unless reinforced by fresh conflict.

2. Kin Revenge Execution

In Milestone 0.5, only the single instigator could kill. In Milestone 1, any eligible adult kin in Degree 1 (son, daughter, brother, father) can execute vengeance:

  • Avenger Selection: When revenge tick fires, the system checks living kin holding hatred ≥ 40.0.
  • Priority: Eldest adult son → brother → father → spouse.
  • Causal Chain: Revenge killing cites both the original murder event AND the parent feud event.

SUBSYSTEM 4: TRUTH PROPAGATION

11. Subsystem 4: Epistemic Belief-Correction Propagation

Establishes a rigorous, bidirectional truth-discovery and reconciliation pipeline. When proof of innocence emerges, corrections cascade through familial networks, enabling authentic peace treaties.

The End-to-End Belief Correction Pipeline

  [ TRUTH DISCOVERED ] ──> Witness testimony, physical evidence, or confession emits REVELATION event
           │
           ▼
[ INSTIGATOR RE-APPRAISAL ] ──> Belief updated (supersedes_belief_id set); Accidental intent recognized
           │                    Emotional Re-Appraisal: REGRET (60-90) & RELIEF (70-100) formed
           ▼
[ FAMILY CASCADE ] ───────────> Instigator shares truth with Degree 1 kin via priority TOLD beliefs
           │                    Kin receive corrective belief; re-appraisal begins
           ▼
[ ASYMMETRIC RE-APPRAISAL ] ──> Sibling with Forgiveness=0.8 accepts truth in 2 ticks
           │                    Brother with Suspicion=0.9 doubts rumor; requires 12 ticks
           ▼
[ RECONCILIATION & PEACE ] ───> Inter-family Hatred drops below 15.0; PEACE treaty ratified

1. Family Truth Transmission Algorithm

When an individual's belief is superseded by a REVELATION event:

  1. The character flags their old belief with supersedes_belief_id.
  2. Forms personal REGRET, RELIEF, and RESPECT memories.
  3. Immediate Family Broadcast: Queues priority communication ticks to all living Degree 1 kin (spouse, children, siblings, parents).
  4. Kin receive a new Belief with source=TOLD, source_character_id=instigator.id, citing the REVELATION event.

2. Observer-Specific Re-Appraisal Speeds

Family members do not reconcile in lockstep. Cognitive traits determine how fast they abandon hatred:

  • High Forgiveness (≥ 0.7): Immediately re-appraises; forms high RELIEF and zero hatred within 1–2 months.
  • Average Citizen (0.4 – 0.6): Fades hatred over 6–12 months as confidence in the correction strengthens.
  • High Suspicion (≥ 0.7): Suspects the confession is a trick or bribe; retains skepticism for up to 24 months until verified by secondary witnesses.

12. Peace Treaty Mechanics & Grievance Closure

In Milestone 1, a peace treaty is not merely a random roll when a feud timer expires. It requires verified social consensus:

Peace Pathway Prerequisite Epistemic Condition Causal DAG Edge Signature Resulting Dynastic State
Truth Revelation Peace Corrective belief accepted by ≥ 75% of living Degree 1 family members; hatred < 15.0. Peace.causes = [Revelation.id, Feud.id] Wrongful feud expunged; mutual trust restored; trade routes reopen.
Negotiated Exhaustion Peace Resource exhaustion, famine, or casualties exceed tolerance; both chiefs agree to truce. Peace.causes = [Feud.id, Raid.id, Famine.id] Cold peace; underlying grievance remains unresolved; trade resumed under caution.
Epistemic Invariant:
Once a grievance is closed via a PEACE event citing REVELATION, that specific historical death event can never again trigger a blood feud across any descendants.
