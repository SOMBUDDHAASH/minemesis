======================================================================
               MINEMESIS: VOXEL CIVILIZATION SIMULATOR
              Standalone Self-Hosted Game (OpenGL 3.3 Core)
======================================================================

Welcome to Minemesis, an entirely standalone 3D voxel civilization game
where an autonomous living historical simulation is coupled directly to a
custom physical 3D voxel world engine.

Zero external engine dependencies. Pure self-hosted procedural technology.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS & SHORTCUTS:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  [W, A, S, D]      Move character (Forward, Left, Backward, Right)
  [Space]           Jump (0.5m automatic stair-stepping built-in)
  [Left Shift]      Sprint
  [Mouse Look]      First-person camera rotation (Pitch & Yaw)
  [Mouse Left]      Mine / Break targeted voxel block
  [Mouse Right]     Place active hand block against targeted face
  [1 to 9]          Select hotbar item
  [Mouse Wheel]     Scroll through hotbar items
  [E]               Speak / Interact with living NPC in front of you
  [R]               Read Ancient Chronicle when targeting Bookshelf / Lectern
  [M]               Continental World Map overlay (settlements & defenses)
  [J]               Historical Chronicle overlay (living events ledger)
  [Esc]             Pause Game Menu / Toggle Cursor / Close Overlays
  [F3]              Toggle Debug & Profiling HUD

----------------------------------------------------------------------
LIVING CIVILIZATION INTERACTIONS:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- NPCs follow real 24-hour daily schedules:
  * 00:00 - 06:00: Sleeping at Home
  * 06:00 - 12:00: Morning Labor at Workshops / Fields / Archives
  * 12:00 - 14:00: Socializing at Village Well / Market
  * 14:00 - 18:00: Afternoon Crafting & Guard Duty
  * 18:00 - 22:00: Evening Worship at Village Shrine
  * 22:00 - 24:00: Returning to Cottage Hearth
- Approach any villager and press [E]:
  * [1] Talk: Context-sensitive dialogue with the citizen
  * [2] Trade: Trade regional goods
  * [3] Gift: Increase affinity (+15)
  * [4] Help: Learn of settlement fortification needs
  * [5] Steal: Attempt pickpocketing (Beware: screams alert guards!)
  * [6] Attack: Engage in combat (Witnesses scream, guards hunt you down!)
- Building Fortifications:
  * Placing stone bricks, cobblestone, or palisade walls near settlements
    increases their Defensibility Score.
  * Villages adapt: morale rises, guards man the battlements, and
    citizens express gratitude for your walls!
- Sleeping:
  * Open Pause Menu [Esc] and select [5] Sleep Until Morning to skip time
    forward to dawn, advancing the simulation clock and historical events.
