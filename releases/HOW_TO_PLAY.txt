======================================================================
               MINEMESIS: VOXEL CIVILIZATION SIMULATOR
              Standalone Self-Hosted Game (OpenGL 3.3 Core)
======================================================================

Welcome to Minemesis, an entirely standalone 3D voxel civilization game
where an autonomous living historical simulation is coupled directly to a
custom physical 3D voxel world engine.

Zero external engine dependencies. Pure self-hosted procedural technology.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLS & SHORTCUTS:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  [W, A, S, D]      Move character (Forward, Left, Backward, Right)
  [Space]           Jump (Automatic 1.1m slope & stair-stepping built-in)
  [Left Shift]      Sprint
  [Mouse Look]      First-person camera rotation (Raw input + smoothing)
  [Mouse Left]      Mine / Break targeted voxel block
  [Mouse Right]     Place active hand block against targeted face
  [1 to 9]          Select hotbar item
  [Mouse Wheel]     Scroll through hotbar items
  [I] / [Tab]       Toggle Minemesis Inventory & Equipment Panel
  [O]               Toggle Settings Menu (Sensitivity, Smoothing, FOV)
  [F]               Toggle Debug Free-Flight Mode (Terrestrial gravity default)
  [E]               Speak / Interact with living NPC in front of you
  [R]               Read Ancient Chronicle when targeting Bookshelf / Lectern
  [M]               Continental World Map overlay (settlements & defenses)
  [J]               Historical Chronicle overlay (living events ledger)
  [T]               Advance Diurnal Time (+6 hours)
  [Esc]             Pause Game Menu / Toggle Cursor / Close Overlays
  [F3]              Toggle Debug & Profiling HUD
  [F4]              Toggle Wireframe Rendering Mode
  [F5]              Toggle Chunk Color Diagnostics

----------------------------------------------------------------------
MOMENT-TO-MOMENT PLAYABILITY PILLARS:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. TERRAIN-CONFORMING CITIZENS:
   - NPCs are physically grounded to the voxel surface with gravity,
     step-up and step-down slope physics. No floating, no burying.
2. TERRESTRIAL PLAYER PHYSICS:
   - Player is bound by continuous gravity (32.0 m/s^2), swept AABB
     voxel collision, and automatic 1.1m step-up on slopes and ledges.
3. MINEMESIS INVENTORY v1:
   - Features standard ItemStack mechanics (up to 64 count), 9-slot
     quick hotbar, 27-slot main storage (3x9 grid), and equipment slots.
4. SOFT CONE AIM ASSIST & INTERACTION UX:
   - Primary raycast is backed by an interaction cone to prevent pixel
     misses when mining block edges.
   - Targeted blocks show a crisp bounding box wireframe outline.
   - Targeted face normal is highlighted with a translucent indicator.
   - Placed blocks display a translucent ghost placement preview box.
5. RESPONSIVE MOUSE DYNAMICS & SETTINGS:
   - GLFW raw mouse input bypasses OS hardware acceleration.
   - Exponential camera delta smoothing eliminates high-DPI mouse jitter.
   - In-game Settings Menu [O] allows on-the-fly sensitivity cycling
     (0.05 - 0.30), FOV adjustments (65° - 95°), and inverted Y-axis.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
LIVING CIVILIZATION INTERACTIONS:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- NPCs follow real 24-hour daily schedules:
  * 00:00 - 06:00: Sleeping at Home (Night Curfew enforced by Guards)
  * 06:00 - 09:00: Morning Gathering at Village Well
  * 09:00 - 17:00: Day Labor at Workshops / Fields / Anvils
  * 17:00 - 21:00: Evening Fellowship at Tavern or Shrine Worship
  * 21:00 - 24:00: Night Lantern Watch & Bedtime
- Approach any villager and press [E]:
  * [1] Talk: Context-sensitive dialogue with the citizen
  * [2] Trade: Trade regional goods
  * [3] Gift: Increase affinity (+15)
  * [4] Help: Learn of settlement fortification needs (+Reputation)
  * [5] Steal: Attempt pickpocketing (Beware: screams alert guards!)
  * [6] Attack: Engage in combat (Kinship grief, succession, outcast branding!)
- Building Fortifications:
  * Placing stone bricks, cobblestone, or palisade walls near settlements
    increases their Defensibility Score.
  * Villages adapt: morale rises, guards patrol the perimeter, and
    citizens express gratitude for your walls!
- Sleeping:
  * Open Pause Menu [Esc] and select [5] Sleep Until Morning to skip time
    forward to dawn, advancing the simulation clock and historical events.
